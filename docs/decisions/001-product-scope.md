Decision 001 — Product Scope

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-08

Decision

Lemma will be developed as a student-run mathematical publication and archive.

The initial product will focus on:

publishing student mathematical work
reading mathematical articles
discovering articles
searching and filtering articles
author profiles
browser-based article creation
peer review
publication workflow
Scale Target

The website should be designed for approximately 100+ published articles by the time the founding students graduate.

This is large enough that article discovery and information architecture must be treated as core product functionality.

Explicitly Deferred

The following are not part of the initial product:

social networking
comments from general readers
arbitrary user-generated JavaScript
server-side Python execution
complex recommendation algorithms
mobile applications

These may be reconsidered later.
